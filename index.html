How do I get one? (one-time, ~2 min)
1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in.
2. Click "Create app". Name it whatever (e.g., "My Playlist Builder").
3. For "Redirect URI", paste exactly:
4. Check the "Web API" box, accept terms, save.
5. Copy the Client ID and paste it above.
Note: in dev mode, Spotify apps only work for accounts you add under "User Management" in your app settings. Add your own Spotify email there.
